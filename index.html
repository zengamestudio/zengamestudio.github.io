About Us

Welcome to Zengamestudio, a passionate game development company specializing in creating delightful and engaging casual games for Western audiences. We believe in the power of fun, intuitive gameplay that can be enjoyed by players of all ages across the globe.

With years of experience in system development, our expertise extends beyond game mechanics into robust backend systems. We specialize in building scalable infrastructure for game publishing, including comprehensive **backend analytics systems** for marketing and in-game performance.

Our current focus is on the European and American markets, ensuring our games resonate with a broad international audience on platforms like Google Play and iOS.

Our Development Philosophy

At Zengamestudio, we blend creativity with cutting-edge technology. We pride ourselves on:

  • Player-Centric Design: We focus on creating games that bring genuine joy and satisfaction to every player.
  • Data-Driven Decisions: Our robust analytics, covering everything from marketing channels and creatives to player sessions and in-game economies, ensure we're always improving our games and strategies.
  • Technical Excellence: We build resilient, high-performance systems capable of supporting our games and millions of players seamlessly.
  • Continuous Improvement: We're always learning, adapting, and embracing new technologies to push the boundaries of casual gaming.
